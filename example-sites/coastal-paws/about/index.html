About

One groomer. One dog at a time.

Coastal Paws is a one-person salon on Manning Street. Eight years of grooming, a soft spot for the anxious ones, and a firm rule that no dog spends the day in a cage waiting.

Gentle by default

Low noise, slow hands, and we stop when a dog needs a minute. Nervous dogs get extra time at no charge.

Honest about coats

If a coat is too matted to brush out kindly, it gets clipped short and you hear about it before, not after.

Two to three hours

Drop off, do the shopping, come back. You get a text the moment they are ready.

Come and meet us first.

Drop in on a Saturday morning with your dog. No booking needed for a nail trim and a hello.

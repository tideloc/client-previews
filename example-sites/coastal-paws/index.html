How It Works

Booking your dog's groom is simple. Here's what to expect from start to finish.

  1. 1

    Book online in minutes

    Choose your service and pick a time that suits you. Available 24 hours a day through our online booking. You will receive a confirmation by email.

  2. 2

    Drop off your dog

    Bring your dog to our Manning Street salon at your booked time. We will check them in, discuss what you want, and take it from there.

  3. 3

    Pick up a happy dog

    We will text when your dog is ready. They will be freshly groomed, fluffy and smelling great, usually within two to three hours.

What every dog gets here

  • No cages for the day. Drop off, do the shopping, pick up in two to three hours.
  • Gentle handling, especially for nervous dogs. Slow, quiet, and we stop when they need a minute. Extra time at no charge.
  • No painful de-matting. If a coat is too far gone to brush out kindly, it's clipped short and you hear about it first.
  • One groomer, start to finish. The person you hand the lead to is the person who does the groom.

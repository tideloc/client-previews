Welcome to Coastal Paws

At Coastal Paws we don't believe your dog is just a pet. We know they are the boss of the house, and we treat them accordingly.

Eight years of grooming, one small salon, and one groomer who does every dog from the first hello to the last brush. We don't rush, we don't cage, and we don't de-mat painfully. If a coat is too far gone, it gets clipped short and you hear about it first.

Whether it's a puppy's first bath or a big kelpie who hates the dryer, this is where they get looked after.

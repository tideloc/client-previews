What to expect

Your first month

  1. 01

    Free 30-minute consult

    We talk about where you are, what's hurt before, and what you actually want. You leave with a plan whether you book or not.

  2. 02

    Assessment session

    Movement, strength baselines, and the numbers we'll measure against. No max-outs, no vomiting into a bucket.

  3. 03

    Programme starts

    Sessions are yours alone. Between them you get a written programme for the days you train without me, if you want them.

Older, not weaker

Most clients are 40 to 65. Bone density, balance and joints are the point, not a six-pack.

Coming back from injury

Cleared by your physio? We build from what you can do today, and we talk to your physio if you want us to.

Never lifted before

Good. No habits to unlearn. Every rep is taught from the first one.

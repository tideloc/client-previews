# Build brief - The Boulevard Cafe on the Lake

2026-10-02 · lead 28236 · skill tideloc-site-build · preview https://staging.tideloc.com.au/cold/the-boulevard-cafe-on-the-lake/v2/

## Who this is for
Yvette Lee, The Boulevard Cafe on the Lake, Victor Harbor SA. Inbound from the home page quiz on 2 Oct 2026 (Site plan, goal "get found and contacted", style "Warm, Friendly, Relaxed, Classic"). In their words: "Licensed café with a passion for local produce and high quality meals. Available for dine in, takeaway, catering and functions in a beautiful location."

This is an EXAMPLE site. It says so in the strip at the top of every page and beside the menu content, and Blake's first email tells her the same.

## What the site has to make happen
Someone in or visiting Victor Harbor finds the cafe on the lake, sees when it is open and what is on the menu, then gets directions or calls. Primary CTA "Get directions". Secondary: call, see the menu, ask about a function.

## Verified live on 2 Oct 2026
- No website. The Google listing's website link points at the Facebook page.
- Facebook page: about 400 photos (Feb 2016 to Oct 2025), bio, phone, email theboulevardcafe8@gmail.com (the address Yvette used).
- Google: 4.3 from 558 reviews; hours 8am to 3pm daily, 3:30pm Thursday.
- Friday and Saturday dinner menu, 5pm to 8pm, posted 3 May 2023.
- Business Victor Harbor 2021 Excellence in Business Awards, Winner, Hospitality (certificate photo on Facebook).

## Brand tokens (sources)
- Palette: #1b6cb0 primary (painted band on the shopfront), #0f2040 ink (wordmark navy), #eb6206 accent (logo wave, verandah chairs), #f4f2ea ground and #e5e6e1 secondary (cream gable panel). All sampled from the shopfront photo.
- Type: Josefin Sans / Nunito Sans. No website fonts exist; the wordmark is light geometric capitals with wide tracking, mapped to Josefin Sans and set the same way.
- Logo: no file. The wordmark is live text and the two-stroke wave is an SVG drawn from the sign.

## Layout, in two lines
The hero is the shopfront sign rebuilt in type: a royal blue band, "CAFÉ ON THE LAKE" in the wordmark's capitals, the wave, and the week's hours on a cream card, with the lake photograph directly under it. The one typographic moment is that headline.

## Imagery
12 first-party photos: 11 from the cafe's Facebook page, 1 (verandah) from the Visit Victor Harbor listing. 0 generated. No stock. The Facebook photos are viewer captures (744 to 1477px wide), not originals.

## Questions for Blake / the client
1. Current breakfast, lunch and drinks menus. Breakfast and lunch on the example come from third-party listings (hey-restaurants, Menulog) and may be out of date.
2. Does Friday and Saturday dinner still run? The menu shown is from May 2023 and Google shows a 3pm close.
3. Hours: Google says 3pm, the tourism listing says 4pm.
4. Logo file, or do we redraw the shopfront mark properly?
5. Original photos, and permission to use the tourism listing's verandah photo.
6. The story for an about page: who owns it, since when, the team. (A January 2021 post celebrates 5 years in business.)
7. Functions and catering: capacity, packages, how bookings work. Is "Book now" on Facebook wired to anything?
8. Instagram account, if there is one. Domain name.
9. The note Yvette flagged in the quiz did not save. Ask her what it was.
10. Does the 2021 award and the Google rating stay on the site?

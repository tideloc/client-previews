# Build brief - The Sands Shellharbour

Preview: https://staging.tideloc.com.au/cold/the-sands-shellharbour/v2/

## Who this is for
The Sands on Shellharbour North Beach, next door to the surf club: two businesses in one building. The Kiosk (casual beachside cafe, open 7 days from 7am, licensed) and The Sands Receptions (event centre, 100 seated / 140 cocktail, in-house catering, weddings to memorials). Their current Wix site splits into two logos on a sand photo and shows no phone or email anywhere.

## What the site has to make happen
Get a local to the kiosk this week (breakfast, Thursday pizza night, the next high tea) and get an event planner to send a reception enquiry. Header carries "Enquire"; hero carries directions plus the "What's on" card.

## Brand tokens (sources)
- Sky blue #1CAADE (their applied Wix header), logo blue #5B96D0 (pixel sample of the blue lockup), sand #FFF0EC ground, ink #282936, grey #94949B labels. The crimson in their Wix theme slots is never rendered on the live site; rejected.
- Type: Forum (their Wix Title/Heading face) + Quicksand (their subhead face) + Nunito Sans standing in for Avenir Light. All set sentence case as they set them.
- Script logo carried in the header wordmark via Forum; both circular logos available.

## Layout, in two lines
Full-bleed aerial hero with their own kiosk line "Life tastes better with a pinch of salt in the air." and a portal-editable "What's on at The Sands" card. Then the sky-blue menu band (all dish names, no invented prices), receptions block with the real 100/140 capacities, the real event list ($55 high teas, sold out flags), photo mosaic, logo-blue location band with their drive-time lines.

## Copy policy
Everything traces to thesands.net.au, their event list, the memorial flyer, or the visitshellharbour listing (phone 0411 476 855 and bookings@thesands.net.au come from the listing; their own site hides both). "Reserve" on pizza night links their real Reservations URL. No menu prices exist, so none are shown.

## Portal
The "What's on" card is the portal price-list block: they can edit events/hours weekly and schedule presets, same as the Tiny Flower House example.

## QC
Independent reviewer (portfolio benchmark): round 1 flagged a duplicated menu band on the kiosk page, a thin events page with cropped posters, a flat contact hero, an overclaimed dogs line and the September high teas presented as "this week". All fixed: kiosk has its own menu treatment, events got a photo hero with reservation CTA and uncropped posters, contact hero sits on the aerial, claims re-sourced, memorial package now an ink band. Gate: PASS.
